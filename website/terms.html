The service

Truck Buddy provides a driver companion app (Android, iOS, web) and a billing portal. The service is in beta: features evolve weekly and occasional downtime is possible. On-duty, hours-of-service, and inspection records in the app are driver aids — they are not a certified ELD.

Subscriptions and billing

  • Plans are per driver, per month, billed monthly through Stripe. Current prices are shown on the pricing page, which is the binding price list.
  • Cancel anytime from the portal; access continues until the end of the paid period. No refunds for partial months.

Messaging

  • You are responsible for having recipients' consent to text them, and for the phone numbers you enter.
  • Do not use the service for spam, unlawful content, or to impersonate anyone.

No reverse-engineering the apps to evade billing, no probing other customers' accounts, no unlawful use. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service or the messaging relays.
