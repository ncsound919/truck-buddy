Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026. Contact: hello@truckbuddy.online.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, used to sign you in (via Supabase) and manage your subscription (via Stripe). We never see or store your card number — payment details go directly to Stripe.
  • Shift data you create: stops, documents you scan, and messages you send through the app. Stored on your device and, for emailed items, transmitted once to the recipient you chose.
  • Location: used on-device only to detect arrival at a stop. It is never uploaded on its own.
  • This site: no analytics, no advertising trackers. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives a standard web request (IP address, browser type) like any CDN.

What we share

  • Resend — delivers emails and carrier-gateway texts you explicitly send. Recipients see your name and unit so messages are never anonymous.
  • Stripe — processes subscriptions. Subject to Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Supabase — hosts authentication and subscription records.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share data with advertisers.

Your rights

Ask for a copy, correction, or deletion of your data at hello@truckbuddy.online. Deleting your account removes your cloud records; data already emailed to recipients you chose cannot be recalled.
