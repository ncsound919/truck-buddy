The product

One flow for the whole day

Every step a driver takes is wired together — so the truck, the paperwork, and the route stay in sync.

✓

Guided inspections

Pre- and post-trip checklists with one-thumb Pass / flag. Issues become voice notes, not clipboard scribbles.

🗣

Hands-free in the cab

Spoken stop-by-stop navigation and status readouts. Keep your hands on the wheel and eyes on the road.

📷

Scan any document

Point at the bill of lading, invoice, or delivery receipt. OCR reads it, extracts the fields, and files it.

❤

Truck health, read out

Coolant, battery, fuel and fault codes read out loud before you leave the yard. Sample diagnostics today — connect your ELD or telematics feed to go live.

📍

Arrival, automatically

Geofencing knows when you’ve arrived and opens the right paperwork for that stop. No manual fumbling.

🛣

Your route, in order

Every stop sequenced with ETA and mileage. Complete one and the next is queued before you pull away.

How it works

From cold start to done — hands-free

  1. 01

    Inspect

    Run the guided pre-trip checklist. Voice confirms the truck is road-ready.

  2. 02

    Drive

    Truck Buddy reads out your next stop, ETA, and any health flags on the move.

  3. 03

    Scan

    Arrive, point at the document, and let OCR verify the details for you.

  4. 04

    Repeat

    The next stop is queued. At end of day, one post-trip check closes the shift.
