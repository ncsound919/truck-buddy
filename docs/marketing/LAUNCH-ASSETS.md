# Truck Buddy — Launch Asset Drafts (Phase 0 / waitlist)

**Status:** DRAFTS for review — NOT published. These support the honest Phase 0 goal:
build a beta waitlist and collect real testimonials. Every asset carries the real ask
("run one demo shift, tell us what broke"), not a paid-signup promise.

**Voice anchor:** "Less paperwork. More road." — specific, concrete, no AI-hype language.

---

## 1. Core angle (one sentence each platform must own)

- **Problem-first:** A trucker shouldn't need a back office to do paperwork, prove a
  detention, or keep a clean inspection record. Truck Buddy puts that office in the cab,
  hands-free.
- **Proof-first:** Point at the BOL. OCR reads it, files it, and the proof is yours before
  you leave the dock.
- **Ask:** Run one demo shift with us in beta. Tell us what breaks.

## 3. LinkedIn (fleet / enterprise angle)

**First line:** Owner-operators lose hours a day to paperwork a back office would handle —
they just don't have one.

Paragraphs (short):
- Truck Buddy pairs a hands-free mobile companion (inspections, OCR document capture,
  geofence arrival, live truck health) with a web portal (load board, dispatch, documents,
  money/factoring). One flow, off the road and on it.
- For small carriers: driver proof baked into every stop, dispatch and fleet health in one
  dashboard.
- We're honest about where we are: beta, demo backend, no claims we haven't shipped. We're
  recruiting the first carriers to run a pilot and shape the product.

**Takeaway / results framing:** none fabricated. State exactly what's real (beta features)
and what's not yet (fleet SSO, live billing) — that candor is the credibility differentiator
for an enterprise buyer.
